# messagent

Message and call your agent in a messaging app, like a friend: Vibe Together's messenger client.

It began as a piece of Ag² (`~/dev/agentics/AGENTS.md`), and now lives in Vibe Together's monorepo, whose `AGENTS.md` (`../../AGENTS.md`) and design hold the framing.

- `design.md`: what is decided, and the questions still open.
- `agents.md`: the page for agents, how a person links Telegram, served at `/apps/messagent/agents.md` and listed in the website's `apps.md`.
- `telegram.md` and `reactions.md`: the extensions for Telegram's own messages and for reacting, served beside it.
- Issues: the plans and the work still to do, in `PotentialHedge/vibetogether`, labelled by part.
  The history with Luis's words and the research is in the first issue of the archived `luiswirth/messagent`.

Work on messagent is paired: Luis guides it deliberately, and its core design is his.
Read the design before working on anything.
